**Style rulings** (accepted by Ali, `docs/adr/0001-advisor-guidance-is-soft-prompt-text.md`):

| # | Point | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Neutrals | `black`, `white`, `gray`, `navy`, `tan`, `beige`, `brown`; `blue` on `jeans` is neutral; `olive` is near-neutral |
| 2 | Accents per outfit | at most one accent color (`red`, `green`, `burgundy`, `pink`, `purple`, `yellow`, `orange`, non-jeans `blue`); shades of one color count as one |
| 3 | `black` + `navy` | allowed |
| 4 | `black` + `brown` | allowed in casual; avoid in formal outfits |
| 5 | Tonal / monochrome | allowed, at most one of the 3 outfits |
| 6 | Patterns per outfit | at most one patterned garment; a patterned accessory only when every garment is `solid` |
| 7 | Belt vs. shoes | belt matches the shoe color family (`brown`/`tan` together, `black` with `black`) |
| 8 | `white` `sneakers` | a neutral that goes with any casual or smart-casual outfit |

Topic examples agreed in planning, to write out in full:
- **Layering:** `blazer` over `shirt`, `polo`, `t-shirt` or `sweater`, never
  over `hoodie`, `sweatshirt` or `tank-top`; the outer layer is usually
  darker or heavier than the one under it.
- **Formality:** no `dress-shoes` with `shorts` or `sweatpants`; a `blazer`
  lifts `jeans` to smart-casual; `sneakers` work with `chinos`; a `tie` only
  with a `shirt`.

## Implementation notes

Implemented at Ali's request, before the `07-architecture.md` edit (blocker 1)
was applied. The code matches the proposed wording. Since ING-061 has since
reworded step 4 to "rain chance, condition code), formality, note, whether the
weather rules are off, and ...", the step 4 edit now applies to that sentence:
the parenthesis becomes "(feels-like, min/max, rain chance, whether rain is
likely per the ≥ 50 % hint, condition code with its label in words)", and the
rest stays as proposed.

- `internal/recommend/advisor_prompt.md` (new, embedded). Sections in the
  required order, taxonomy values in backticks, all 8 style rulings. The
  baseline eval's low scores led to four sharper rules:
  - Rain needs a protective outer layer: a `coat` or `jacket`, not a `blazer`
    or `vest` on its own.
  - Avoid `dress-shoes` and `loafers` in rain.
  - Dress for the occasion in the note, not above it.
  - Prefer a removable layer when the temperature range is wide or unknown.

  These sit within the agreed topics (weather sense, formality); no new topic
  was added.
- `internal/recommend/picker.go`:
  - `contractPrompt` is the old constant without the three style lines.
  - `systemPrompt` is the contract followed by the embedded guidance.
  - `wmoLabels`, `conditionLine` and `rainLine` are new.
  - The user prompt's `weather code (WMO)` line became
    `rain likely today: yes|no|unknown` and `condition: <label> (WMO <code>)`.
- `internal/recommend/advisor_test.go`:
  - The backticked-values-are-taxonomy check.
  - System prompt = contract + guidance, with the section order checked.
  - Condition labels for mapped, unmapped and nil codes.
  - Rain line yes/no/unknown, and the old line gone.
- `internal/recommend/picker_test.go`: the `weather code (WMO): 61` assertion
  is now `condition: rain (WMO 61)` plus `rain likely today: no`.
